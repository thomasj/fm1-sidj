SiDJ

Turn the M-VAVE FM-1 into a C64 deck. Forty years of SID music, from 1980s game themes to this year's demo compo winners, play on a cycle-exact Commodore 64 inside the FM-1, and every voice is yours to remix live: transpose from the keys, mute and solo, swap waveforms, sweep the filter, ride the tempo and a vinyl fader.

On the FM-1

A tune you can play with

Each voice gets its own scope, note, waveform, envelope and pulse width, and the filter is laid out beneath. Whatever you change shows up in gold, with REMIX at the top, and two buttons put it all back.

What it does

Made for playing live

The whole of HVSC

Tens of thousands of tunes: game classics, demo bangers, today's compo winners. Each plays on the 6581 or 8580 it was written for and stops where HVSC says it ends.

Remix every voice

Hold a key on the FM-1 or a MIDI keyboard and the voice follows, in time. Mute, solo, swap waveforms, bend envelopes and pulse width, push the cutoff up to 16×, give each voice its own LFO, and ride the tempo and a vinyl fader, from the panel or a controller's CCs. Two buttons put it all back.

Real SID, USB audio

Drive a SidStation, TherapSID, TeensyROM or USBSID-Pico over ASID, remix included, alone or alongside the emulation. Over the same cable the FM-1 is a driverless audio interface: the mix, or each voice on its own channel, straight into your DAW.

Try it first

The browser emulator runs the very same firmware, panel and all. Drop a .sid on it and play.

Under the hood

A real C64 in there

Not a player that approximates. A Commodore 64 emulated cycle by cycle, with the SID engine the C64 scene measures itself against.

Cycle-exact C64

A 6510 with IRQ and NMI, both CIAs, the VIC's raster and bad lines. Every I/O access lands on its own cycle, so stable-raster code, NMI sample players and multi-speed tunes up to 38× run as written.

reSID inside

A fixed-point port of reSID 0.16, bit-exact with the original, as a 6581 or an 8580. The 8580's floating waveform DAC is there too, so tunes that play samples with the test bit sound as they should.

No aliasing

Pulse, sawtooth and the combined waveforms are averaged over every cycle of each sample instead of picked once a sample: narrow pulses stay steady and edges stay clean.

Measured, not guessed

The SID writes match libsidplayfp's own C64 at a median of 99.99 % (PSID) and 99.92 % (RSID) over random HVSC tunes, and the audio load is measured on the FM-1 with the heaviest tunes found: no dropouts, by design. The details.

Get started

Three steps

1

Install SiDJ

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Those browsers can talk MIDI to the FM-1.
  2. Switch the FM-1 on and connect it straight to the computer with a USB data cable. Close any other app that uses MIDI.
  3. Press Install and allow MIDI when the browser asks. Keep the cable in while it writes, which takes a minute or two.

Details

        

        
The installer works from M-VAVE's own firmware (V16 or V15), from Felucca, from X0X and from other open firmwares built on Felucca's
          updater. If a write is interrupted, the FM-1 stays in update mode: reconnect it and press Install again. If the FM-1 no longer
          starts, it falls back by itself. Two failed starts give a safe mode where PLAY tries again, and four give the chip's own update
          mode, which OCT− and OCT+ held for 5 seconds also reach. From there, the README's tools/fm1_rescue.sh or
          M-VAVE's FM-1 Transporter puts firmware back.

        
SiDJ is free software with no warranty. Installing custom firmware is at your own risk.

              choose the unmodified FM-1.fwsc. An FM-1.fwsc of V15 you already have works too. The browser checks it here and sends it nowhere else. The songs on the FM-1 are not kept,

Play and remix

        

          
SEQ switches between the song list and the player. A & B means hold A, then press B;
            a voice button is HOME, SAVE or ARP. The full manual is in the
            README, and the emulator lets you try every control.

          
Song list

          

            
SELECTmove through the list

            
OCT−eight songs up

            
OCT+eight songs down

            
PLAYplay the song

            
PRESETSthe subsong

            
KNOB 1the SID chip: AUTO (the tune's), 6581 or 8580

            
KNOB 2output: emulated SID, ASID, or both

            
KNOB 3USB audio: the mix, or the mix and each voice

            
SAVEkeep these settings

          

          
Playing

          

            
PLAYplay or pause

            
REC&PLAYplay from the start

            
SELECTthe next or previous song

            
PRESETSthe subsong

            
ALGORITHMoutput: emulated SID, ASID, or both

            
MASTERthe level at the audio jack

            
SEQback to the song list

          

          
Voices and keys

          

            
HOMEvoice 1

            
SAVEvoice 2

            
ARPvoice 3

            
the keysplay the selected voice

            
OCT−the keys an octave down, three at most

            
OCT+the keys an octave up, three at most

            
FX&OCT−the keys transpose the tune

            
FX&OCT+the keys set the pitch

            
MIDI keyboardplays as the keys, over USB or MIDI IN: channels 1–3 a voice each, others the selected one

            
MIDI CCsthe whole remix from a controller: CC 70, 72, 73 and 75–88 a voice (by channel, as the keys), 71, 74 and 102–108 the filter and the deck (the table is in SiDJ over MIDI)

            
RECmute or unmute the voice

            
voice&RECsolo the voice

          

          
Waveforms

          

            
voice&FXpulse

            
voice&SELtriangle

            
voice&ENVsawtooth

            
voice&LFOnoise

            
voice&EDITsync on or off

            
voice&GLOring modulation on or off

          

          
Filter

          

            
SEL&HOMEvoice 1 through the filter, or not

            
SEL&SAVEvoice 2 through the filter, or not

            
SEL&ARPvoice 3 through the filter, or not

            
SEL&LFOlow-pass on or off

            
SEL&EDITband-pass on or off

            
SEL&GLOhigh-pass on or off

            
SEL&OCT+the cutoff knob scales the tune's cutoff

            
SEL&OCT−the cutoff knob adds to it

          

          
Knob pages

          

            
FXpulse width, octave, fine tune

            
ENVattack, decay, sustain, release

            
SELcutoff, resonance

            
LFOrate, depth, target, shape: an LFO per voice

            
GLOtempo 25–150 %, a ±8 % vinyl fader

          

          
Back to the tune

          

            
OCT−OCT+pressed together: everything back
